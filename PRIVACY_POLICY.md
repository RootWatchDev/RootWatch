# Privacy Policy for RootWatch

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**App Name:** RootWatch  
**App ID:** ADJ9esh5hQm_THRuF_6AcQ  

RootWatch is committed to protecting the privacy of all community members. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when using the RootWatch application within the Root platform.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and Process
RootWatch is designed as a privacy-first, real-time synchronized playback tool. We do not track, collect, or store personal user data.

- **Playback & Queue State:** Video URLs, play/pause statuses, and playback timestamps are processed transiently in memory to synchronize video playback between connected room participants.
- **Session Presence & User Display:** Temporary user identifiers (such as your Root User ID and display name) provided by the Root SDK are used solely to indicate spectator presence and DJ host permissions in the active room.
- **Reactions:** Interactive emoji reactions sent during a session are ephemeral and broadcast in real time to room members without persistent storage.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services
- **YouTube:** When watching YouTube content, video playback is handled via YouTube's standard embedded player. Use of YouTube content is subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Direct Video Streams:** When streaming direct MP4/HLS links, the media is streamed directly from the host provider to the viewer's client without intermediary proxy caching.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security
- RootWatch does not maintain external databases of user profiles, browsing histories, or private messages.
- All real-time communication is encrypted and securely routed via the official Root Application SDK infrastructure.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
RootWatch does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be reflected directly within the application and store listing.

---

### 6. Contact Us
For questions regarding this policy or RootWatch, please reach out via the Root Developers community.
