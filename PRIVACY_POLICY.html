Privacy Policy for RootWatch

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 | App ID: ADJ9esh5hQm_THRuF_6AcQ

1. Information We Collect and Process

RootWatch is designed as a privacy-first, real-time synchronized playback tool. We do not track, collect, or sell personal user data.

  • Playback & Queue State: Video URLs, play/pause statuses, and playback timestamps are processed transiently in memory to synchronize video playback between connected room participants.
  • Session Presence: Temporary user identifiers (Root User ID and display name) are used solely to indicate spectator presence and host permissions in the active room.
  • Reactions: Floating emojis sent during a session are ephemeral and broadcast in real time without persistent storage.

2. Third-Party Services

When watching YouTube content, video playback is handled via YouTube's standard embedded player in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Security

All real-time communication is encrypted and securely routed via the official Root Application SDK infrastructure.

4. Contact

For questions or support, contact the developer via the Root Developers community.
